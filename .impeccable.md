## Design Context

### Users
Mobile players exploring alternate lives. They set a world, era, identity, and aspiration before starting a generated life.

### Brand Personality
Mythic, contemplative, inviting. The home screen should make a new life feel like stepping into a richly illustrated world.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use `public/assets/game/world-home-reference.png` as the visual source of truth for the home screen: a dense celestial atlas, ink-painted floating worlds, deep night blues, antique gold, jade light, and restrained luminous accents. Preserve its composition and content hierarchy at the 393 × 852 CSS viewport.

### Design Principles
- Keep the supplied atlas artwork full-bleed and preserve its title, map, settings, selection rows, and main action placement.
- Keep all controls real and accessible; overlay the world input, random-world action, era/identity/aspiration choices, and start action on the artwork.
- Keep responsive scaling proportional to the reference and avoid adding UI chrome that is absent from it.
- Preserve the mobile runtime, live device status bar, keyboard-aware inputs, and existing life flow.
